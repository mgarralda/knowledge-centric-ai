# ICLA Conformance Coverage Matrix

This matrix maps the proposed invariants in Sect. 8.1 of the paper to the
evidence available in the companion artifact set. It is a coverage and
evidentiary map, not a certification result, maturity score, or substitute for
the invariant definitions in the paper.

The **Machine-checkable clauses** column describes only the deterministic
clauses exercised by `icla-spec 0.1.0`. **Supporting artifacts** are
non-normative schemas and constructed records. **Executable tests** identify
the checks that replay positive and negative paths. The final column preserves
the semantic or institutional judgment that the companion deliberately does
not automate. Conformance profiles are omitted because their cumulative
membership is derivable from Table 7 of the paper and from
`ConformanceProfile` in the reference implementation.

The combined trace scope is bounded: `oauth-042` exercises the ICLA-Governed
resolution-to-succession path and the ICLA-11 pre-institutional boundary;
`auth-evolution-formation` exercises governed formation from a submitted
proposal through identity assignment, complete initial CKC v1 append, a formed
state without an active pointer, and separate activation. Automatic discovery,
promotion quality, organizational effectiveness, production integration, and
distributed-operation properties remain outside machine assessment.

## Interpretation

- A linked artifact demonstrates representation and traceability within the
  declared constructed scope; it does not by itself establish conformance.
- A linked test demonstrates deterministic executable behavior for the clause
  it exercises; it does not automate the governed-judgment remainder.
- A profile claim must still name its assessment scope and distinguish
  machine-checked results from governed judgments, as required by Sect. 8.2 of
  the paper.
- The paper remains authoritative if this matrix and the invariant wording ever
  diverge.
